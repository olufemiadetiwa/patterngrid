01

Where is outlet demand outpacing the stock that is actually available to serve it?

The deliverable as a sample report page on demo data. Use the pages and figures; nothing is sent or saved.

02

How it is built

Three stages, each leaving something a reader can check: the reconciliation, the definition, the evidence behind the figure.

Stock modelStage 1

Warehouse stock, in-transit quantities and outlet-level availability share one product and location model. Units and assumptions are written down alongside the sample data.

Sales viewStage 2

Sell-in and sell-out are compared by territory and outlet, with the difference shown explicitly.

Exception queueStage 3

A rule flags outlets where demand exceeds available stock and assigns each exception to a review owner.

03

What it is designed to enable

Outcomes the system is built to make possible. Because this scenario runs on sample data, none is a measured result.

LimitationWhat this does not prove

Availability is only as good as the last count. Outlets not visited in the week are excluded and the exclusion is shown rather than estimated.
