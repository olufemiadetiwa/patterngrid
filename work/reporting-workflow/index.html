01

Can a recurring management report be prepared with less manual effort while a person still approves what is published?

The deliverable as a sample report page on demo data. Use the pages and figures; nothing is sent or saved.

02

How it is built

Three stages, each leaving something a reader can check: the reconciliation, the definition, the evidence behind the figure.

Rule-based checksStage 1

Rules confirm that inputs are complete, periods match and totals reconcile before any draft is produced.

AI-assisted summaryStage 2

A separate, labelled step drafts commentary from the checked figures. Its output is a proposal, not a decision.

Review and approved outputStage 3

A named reviewer sees the draft, its sources and the check results, then approves or returns it. Only the approved version is published.

03

What it is designed to enable

Outcomes the system is built to make possible. Because this scenario runs on sample data, none is a measured result.

LimitationWhat this does not prove

The AI step drafts commentary from checked figures only. A missing source stops the item at review; nothing is released without its evidence.
