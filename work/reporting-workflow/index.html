A reporting workflow with human review

An illustrative workflow that prepares a management report, checks its inputs and routes the draft for review.

Built with sample data to show how we think. It is not a client engagement and does not claim a business result.

The question

Can a recurring management report be prepared with less manual effort while a person still approves what is published?

The deliverable, working. Use the controls; nothing is sent or saved.

Sample inputs
  • Approved weekly metrics from the BI model
  • Finance ledger for the reporting period
  • A reviewer list with named owners
  • Agreed checks: period match, completeness, reconciliation
Approach
  1. Rule-based checks. Rules confirm that inputs are complete, periods match and totals reconcile before any draft is produced.
  2. AI-assisted summary. A separate, labelled step drafts commentary from the checked figures. Its output is a proposal, not a decision.
  3. Review and approved output. A named reviewer sees the draft, its sources and the check results, then approves or returns it. Only the approved version is published.

Select → Design → Prototype → Evaluate → Integrate — the Business Intelligence approach.

How the data moves

Scheduled trigger → approved metrics → checks → proposed draft → accountable review → approved release, recorded.

Designed to enable
Less manual assembly of recurring reports
A visible record of checks and approvals
Clear separation of automated, assisted and human steps

What the system is built to make possible, not a measured result.

Limitation

What this does not prove

The AI step drafts commentary from checked figures only. A missing source stops the item at review; nothing is released without its evidence.

Technology (illustrative options)

Rule-based checks in SQL · An approved model service for the labelled drafting step (option) · Power Automate-style approval routing (option)
