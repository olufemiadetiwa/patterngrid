01

Three functions report performance from three systems. Leadership needs one view that each function can trace back to its own records.

The deliverable as a sample report page on demo data. Use the pages and figures; nothing is sent or saved.

02

How it is built

Three stages, each leaving something a reader can check: the reconciliation, the definition, the evidence behind the figure.

Source reconciliationStage 1

We line up finance, sales and operations records by reporting period and by what they describe (customer, product, outlet). Where they disagree, the difference is listed, not quietly fixed.

Metric dictionaryStage 2

Any reader can open an executive metric and see its definition, its period rule, its owner and where the figure came from.

Management view and decision briefStage 3

A single management view presents the agreed metrics. A decision brief pairs each exception with evidence and a proposed next action.

03

What it is designed to enable

Outcomes the system is built to make possible. Because this scenario runs on sample data, none is a measured result.

LimitationWhat this does not prove

Connecting the sources does not prove a cause. The view shows where to look; the decision and its follow-up stay with the named owner.
