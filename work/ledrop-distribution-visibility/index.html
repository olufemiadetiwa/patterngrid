LedropConnecting sales and field data to improve distribution visibility

Sales and field data held in two platforms, with inconsistent product and customer names, became one reporting foundation in Power BI, giving Ledrop clearer visibility into sales, distribution gaps, menu listings and reported out-of-stock issues.

01

The problem

Ledrop’s sales and field operations data were held across two platforms: Uwanjani and QuickBooks. Each captured important business information, but differences in how products and customers were recorded made it difficult to connect the data and produce consistent reports.

The same product or customer could appear under different names, which complicated matching records, consolidating sales and comparing field observations with business transactions. Beyond sales reporting, management needed to understand where products were missing, where menu listings could be improved, and which outlets had reported out-of-stock situations.

The main challenges
Disconnected data sourcesSales and field information lived in separate platforms, making consolidated analysis difficult.
Inconsistent product namesVariations in product nomenclature complicated matching and reporting across systems.
Inconsistent customer namesDifferences in customer and outlet naming made it harder to connect sales records with field observations.
Limited distribution visibilityThe business needed a structured way to identify outlets where relevant Ledrop products were absent.
Limited menu listing visibilityField observations needed to become clear reporting on missing product listings.
Fragmented out-of-stock reportingReported stock gaps needed organising so teams could see which outlets and products required follow-up.
02

What was built

Pattern Grid developed a Business Intelligence solution that integrated data from Uwanjani and QuickBooks, standardised key business records, and translated the combined information into Power BI reporting.

Integrate the data through AzureDelivered

Azure handled the extract, transform and load process, with Azure Data Factory pipelines bringing both platforms into a structured reporting environment: a repeatable way to prepare sales and field information for analysis.

Standardise product and customer namingDelivered

Product nomenclature was aligned so variations referring to the same product analyse consistently, and customer and outlet names were harmonised so sales transactions connect to field records without splitting one customer or product into several reporting categories.

Build consistent sales reportingDelivered

Reporting structures and measures were developed to analyse sales by customer, product and reporting period on the standardised data.

Develop distribution and menu-listing gap analysisDelivered

Field observations were turned into reporting on distribution gaps (outlets where relevant Ledrop products were not observed), menu-listing gaps (products missing from outlet menus) and competitor-based opportunities (observed competitor products compared with the expected Ledrop equivalents).

Organise out-of-stock reportingDelivered

Out-of-stock observations captured through field activity were organised so the products and outlets associated with availability issues are easy to identify.

Present the insights in Power BIDelivered

Sales and field analyses were brought into Power BI dashboards that let users explore performance and gaps by product, customer or outlet, and reporting period.
