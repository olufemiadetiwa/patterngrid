# Pattern Grid — creative direction (Phase 2)

## Position

Pattern Grid is a data, intelligence and applied AI partner. The site should read as a firm that engineers things: precise, quiet, exact about what it shows. African-founded is stated plainly on About and visible in the photography and the examples; it is not the visual motif.

## The idea: the grid resolves

The mark is nine cells with one lit. That is the whole story of the work: many scattered records, one that matters, found by putting things in order. Three uses, and no others:

1. **Composition.** Sections sit on a visible twelve-column grid with hairline rules; diagrams are built from cells and connectors on the same module.
2. **State.** One lit cell marks the current stage, the selected item or the exception. Mint on dark, indigo on light.
3. **Narrative.** Fragmented records become one aligned record; five stages resolve into one decision.

No grid wallpaper, no decorative dots.

## Typography

Geist stays: it is a precise geometric grotesque, already self-hosted, and changing typefaces would not create authorship. The change is in how it is set.

| Role | Setting |
|---|---|
| Display (h1) | Geist 600, −0.045em, 0.96 line height, 56 to 104 px fluid |
| Section heading | Geist 600, −0.035em, 1.02, 34 to 60 px |
| Sub-heading | Geist 600, −0.02em, 21 to 28 px |
| Lead | Geist 400, 19 to 22 px, 1.5 |
| Body | Geist 400, 16 to 18 px, 1.6 |
| Annotation | **Geist Mono** 500, 12 to 13 px, uppercase, 0.06em: eyebrows, stage numbers, figure captions, data labels |
| Data | Geist Mono, tabular |

Minimum text size 12 px.

## Composition

Light-first. Porcelain pages with white figures; obsidian is reserved for the system map, the Intelligence Canvas and the final call to action, so dark means "this is the machinery". Sections alternate a wide figure, a dense module and a quiet text passage. Hairline rules instead of card shadows; radius 4 to 10 px; no glass, no gradients, no blobs.

## Imagery

Diagrams first: architecture, flows, matrices, definitions. Photography only where a person or place is the subject: founder, About, industries, case-study context (captioned as context). No photographs of laptops in heroes.

Every demonstration carries the caption `ILLUSTRATIVE · SAMPLE DATA` in mono. Nothing is drawn with window chrome, a product name or a login.

## Motion

Motion explains a change of state and then stops. Control feedback 150 ms; state changes 220 to 320 ms; one finite signal pass through the hero system map (about six seconds, once). Nothing loops. Reduced motion shows final states at once. Every interactive figure is complete and legible as a still.

## Voice

Problem → capability → approach → outcome. Short sentences, concrete nouns, no "unlock", no "transformation", no superlatives, no numbers the records do not hold.
