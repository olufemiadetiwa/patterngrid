About Pattern Grid

Clarity isour purpose.

An African-founded data and AI consultancy with an international outlook, led by Precious Chinenye Celestine. We help organisations build the foundations for better decisions.

Meet the founder Our approach →

Pattern Grid helps organisations build the foundations for better decisions, bringing business understanding, technical care and practical thinking to the same table.

Every business collects information in its finance, sales, operations and customer systems. The hard part is making those numbers agree, connect and mean something. Pattern Grid exists to help you do that, in the systems and in the way your teams work.

We start with what you need to understand, then work back through the reporting, the data and the systems needed to support it.

HOW WE WORK

Four commitments.

01

Begin with the decision.

02
