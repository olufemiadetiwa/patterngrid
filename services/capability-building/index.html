What this is

Train your people on the work they actually do.

A new system stays dependent on whoever built it unless your team has practised on it, questioned it and taken it over. A slide deck or a course certificate does not show that someone can do the job.

Pattern Grid shapes capability building around roles and real business responsibilities. Leaders learn to interpret and challenge information. Analysts practise modelling, analysis and reporting. Technical teams work through the tasks needed to maintain the platform. The programme combines a clear starting baseline, guided practice and evidence of independent use.

This is useful when
  • Reports are widely available but poorly understood
  • Knowledge sits with one specialist
  • A delivery project needs a proper handover
  • Teams need practical data and AI skills tied to their roles
What you are working towards

A team that can do the agreed tasks without us, knows what it is responsible for, and keeps the routines going.

What you receive

We agree the exact deliverables with you around your priorities, your systems and where you are starting from.

Role and skills baseline

A view of the tasks each group needs to perform and the current capability gaps, judged by watching people do the task, not by asking how confident they feel.

Tailored learning pathways

A role-specific sequence of topics and practice linked to the team's responsibilities, existing tools and the outcomes the organisation needs to sustain.

Guided practical labs

Exercises using agreed business examples or suitable sample data, with clear tasks, worked guidance and opportunities to practise the skills required in daily work.

Practical assessment pack

Work-based tasks and review criteria that help demonstrate what participants can perform independently and where further practice or support is needed.

Playbooks and handover materials

Usable reference guides, templates and operating instructions that connect the learning to the actual reports, data processes or workflows the team will own.

Adoption and reinforcement plan

An agreed plan for champions, clinics or follow-up reviews where included in scope, using observed use and feedback to guide further support.

Learning tracks shaped around each role

Each role learns the tools it will actually use. This is training for your team as part of an engagement, not a public course catalogue.

Leaders and business users Data literacy, KPI interpretation, Excel and Power BI consumption, with practical discussion of AI-assisted work where relevant.
Analysts Power BI, Power Query, DAX, SQL and Excel, applied to modelling, analysis and clear reporting.

How we would measure success

Agreed with you before work starts. These are the measures, not results we are claiming.

  • Agreed tasks completed independently
  • Quality against the task rubric
  • Use of the delivered tools
  • Issues resolved using the playbook
  • Reliance on external help over an agreed observation period
Common questions
Is this only for technical teams?

No. Leaders, analysts, business users and technical teams need different forms of capability, each connected to their responsibilities.

Can we use our own examples?

Yes, where the examples and data are suitable and approved. Sample data can be used where confidentiality or access makes that more appropriate.

Can this run alongside implementation?

Yes. We can build the practice, documentation and handover into the delivery itself, so your team is ready to take over as the system is finished.
