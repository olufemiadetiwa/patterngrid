What this is

Make the path from source to answer dependable.

When reporting depends on manual exports, copied spreadsheets and undocumented fixes, every change creates another opportunity for the numbers to drift. The result is a fragile process that becomes harder to maintain as the business grows.

We build the data pipelines and platform behind your reporting. We connect the sources you agree, record every step the data goes through, check it as it arrives, and shape it so reports and automation can use it. We also agree who keeps it running once we have left.

This is useful when
  • ERP, CRM and finance data remain separate
  • Reporting relies on repeated file handling
  • Pipelines fail without clear ownership
  • The current data estate cannot support a new analytical requirement
What you are working towards

Data that arrives on schedule, has been checked, shows where it came from, and has a named person keeping it running.

Worked example

Can sales, inventory and customer information support the same commercial view?

  1. 01Product, customer and period keys are agreed as the shared identifiers across the three sources.
  2. 02A stock record for an outlet code that does not exist in the customer master fails validation and is held in the exception queue with a named owner.
  3. 03Once corrected, the record is revalidated and the curated model exposes one documented commercial view that BI can consume.

What you receive

We agree the exact deliverables with you around your priorities, your systems and where you are starting from.

Source and integration blueprint

A documented view of the systems, data interfaces and refresh requirements, including access dependencies and the rules for bringing information together.

Implemented data pipelines

Repeatable ingestion and transformation workflows for the agreed sources, with scheduling, failure handling and clear ownership of the operating process.

Data warehouse or lakehouse

Data structures designed around the required analysis, with consistent keys, documented transformations and a clear path from raw inputs to usable outputs.

Data quality controls

Checks for agreed completeness, validity and reconciliation rules, with exceptions recorded visibly and routed to an owner for investigation and resolution.

Who can see what, and where each number comes from

A view of who can access which information and how important outputs connect to their source data, transformations and agreed ownership responsibilities.

Operating guide and runbooks

Deployment guidance, monitoring definitions and practical runbooks so the operating team can understand routine tasks, investigate failures and manage agreed changes.

How we would measure success

Agreed with you before work starts. These are the measures, not results we are claiming.

  • Completed loads within the required freshness window
  • Reconciliation variance
  • Unresolved exceptions
  • Recovery time
  • Completeness of runbooks and operating ownership
Common questions
Can you work with our existing systems?

Yes. We start from the systems you already have. We would only suggest replacing or migrating one when the requirement and the business case justify it.

Will the data update in real time?

Refresh frequency depends on the decision, the source interfaces and the operating cost. A daily or scheduled process may be appropriate; real time is not assumed.

Who maintains the platform?

That is agreed with you during the engagement and written into the handover: who owns it, who monitors it, who supports it.
